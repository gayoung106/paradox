*Note.* UPB = unethical pro-organizational behavior; OCBI = organizational citizenship behavior directed at individuals. Y8_4 states, “My supervisor provides fair opportunities (e.g., promotion and work assignments) to all employees in the department.” Y11_1 states, “My supervisor makes fair and balanced decisions in handling work.” The sensitivity models retained the same sample, latent constructs, structural paths, and controls for gender, age, and organizational sector; only the designated indicator was removed. The structural models in Panel A included perceived equity, perceived inclusion, organizational identification, ethical leadership, OCBI, and UPB simultaneously. Panel B was estimated separately by adding a double-mean-centered, matched-pairs product-indicator latent interaction. The original and Y8_4-exclusion interaction models used five product indicators; the Y11_1-exclusion model used four because ethical leadership retained four indicators. Entries are standardized coefficients (β). The p values are Wald tests associated with the corresponding unstandardized estimates. Formal BCa intervals were available for the original specification (e.g., OI × EL: 95% BCa CI [−.148, −.022]) but were not generated for the two item-exclusion specifications; they are therefore not imputed here. Panel C reports descriptive products of standardized component paths. Bootstrap confidence intervals for the item-exclusion indirect effects were not estimated, so Panel C does not retest their statistical significance. All models converged. The three specifications used N = 2,020.

**Sensitivity conclusion.** The directions and approximate magnitudes of the focal structural estimates, including the negative OI × EL interaction, were retained after either item was excluded. Because bootstrap confidence intervals were not estimated for the two deletion specifications, the result is classified as **PARTIALLY ROBUST**, rather than as a complete inferential replication.
