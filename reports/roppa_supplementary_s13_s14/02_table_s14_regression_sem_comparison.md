*Note.* OI = organizational identification; OCBI = organizational citizenship behavior directed at individuals; UPB = unethical pro-organizational behavior. Regression-based estimates are the designated primary analyses and latent-variable SEM estimates are robustness checks. B denotes an unstandardized coefficient and β denotes a standardized coefficient; their magnitudes must not be compared directly. The regression analyses did not come from one integrated model: Equity → OI and Inclusion → OI were estimated in separate mediation models; the OI outcome comparison used a simultaneous standardized path model; direct associations were estimated in simultaneous HC3 regressions; indirect associations were estimated in the simultaneous composite-score mediation model; and moderation and conditional indirect associations were estimated in the designated moderation and separate PROCESS Model 14 specifications. The latent structural SEM included equity and inclusion simultaneously and controlled for gender, age, and organizational sector. SEM confidence intervals are BCa intervals based on the saved bootstrap results. The Wald test and BCa interval disagree for Inclusion → UPB; this result is therefore classified as inference-sensitive rather than simply significant. The high-ethical-leadership inclusion indirect association is significant in the regression model but not in the latent SEM. The negative OI × ethical leadership interaction is supported under both approaches, even though the high-ethical-leadership simple slope differs in statistical significance. N = 2,020 throughout.

ᵃ The values are reported identically in the current manuscript and Supplementary Table S3, but an exact saved numerical output reproducing these confidence limits was not located. They remain `UNVERIFIED` in the coefficient traceability file; no replacement values were inferred.
